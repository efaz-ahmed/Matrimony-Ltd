Frequently Asked

Questions, answered.

For wedding customers and prospective investors alike. Here's what you need to know.

How does Matrimony Ltd contribute to wedding hall elegance?
Matrimony Ltd provides wedding hall venues and services aimed at creating a memorable and seamless experience for couples and guests through elegant spaces and professional event management.
What is the Nikah Service offered by Matrimony Ltd?
The Nikah Service facilitates Islamic marriages with qualified Imams ensuring the ceremony is conducted according to Islamic traditions, creating a culturally rich, meaningful experience.
Tell us about the Wedding Cake Service.
The Cake Service enhances celebrations with delicious, beautifully crafted cakes, including traditional and modern designs that add a sweet touch to weddings and special occasions.
What is the Pre-Marriage Fiqh Course, and how does it benefit couples?
The Pre-Marriage Fiqh Course educates couples about Islamic principles and practices related to marriage. It aims to provide a strong foundation for a prosperous and harmonious marital life.
Are there integrated packages combining multiple services?
Yes. Matrimony Ltd offers integrated packages that can include combinations of wedding hall bookings, Nikah Service, Cake Service and the Pre-Marriage Fiqh Course.
How can someone join the company as an investor?
Visit the Contact page, fill out the form with relevant details, and include a message expressing interest in joining as an investor. A team member will respond shortly.
How does the future MatriCare project ensure compassionate care?
The future care home project is designed to provide a caring and supportive environment for seniors, creating a home-like atmosphere focused on wellbeing, dignity and comfort.
